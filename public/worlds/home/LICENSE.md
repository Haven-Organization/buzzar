# Buzzar Home (default Home World)

`home.glb`, `home-preview.jpg` and `home-avatar.png` are built by `scripts/buzzar/home/compose-home.mjs` and
`scripts/buzzar/finish-world.mjs` entirely from public-domain (CC0 1.0) material:

- Trees, bushes, flowers, campfire, logs and rocks: [Kenney Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit) by Kenney (CC0), recoloured.
- Grass (`grass_ground`) and paving (`cobblestone_floor_01`) textures and the sky (`kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky`): [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) (CC0).
- The avatars offered on the pedestals are bundled separately in `public/avatars` (Polygonal Mind, CC0).

The scene itself (layout, mirror, pedestals, seats) is part of Buzzar (Apache-2.0).
